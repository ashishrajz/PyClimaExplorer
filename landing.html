Core Capabilities
Built for Climate
Explorers

Real-time visualization of multi-dimensional climate datasets. From raw numbers to compelling, interactive stories.

🗺️
Global Heatmaps
Interactive choropleth maps. Select any time-slice, pan, zoom, and inspect individual grid cells on a live globe.
📈
Time-Series Analysis
Click anywhere on the map to extract a time-series at that lat/lon. Trend lines and seasonal decomposition included.
🗂️
NetCDF Native
Powered by Xarray. Upload .nc files directly — temperature, precipitation, wind speed auto-detected.
⚔️
Comparison Mode
Side-by-side dataset comparison. Overlay 1990 vs 2020 anomalies. Diff maps reveal how fast change is happening.
🌐
3D Globe View
PyDeck-powered 3D rendering. Rotate the earth, see atmospheric layers stack for public outreach.
🧭
Guided Story Mode
Automated tours highlighting climate anomalies — 2003 European heatwave, Arctic sea-ice collapse.
Workflow Guide
Two Ways to
Explore Data

Dive into the granular raw variables yourself, or let our AI guide you through localized, data-driven climate narratives.

01. Interactive Data Explorer
1
Upload .NC File
Upload standard NetCDF simulation files (like ERA5 or CESM) directly into the app.
2
Select Variable
Toggle between auto-detected parameters such as Temperature, Precipitation, or Wind.
3
Set Time Slice
Scrub through the interactive timeline to pinpoint specific dates or average out decades.
4
Visualize
Instantly render your selections as dynamic 2D heatmaps or immersive 3D globes.
02. AI Story Mode
1
Enable Story Mode
Activate the AI Storyteller feature from the main dashboard control panel to begin.
2
Select Location
Click anywhere on the interactive map or 3D globe to pinpoint a specific geographic region.
3
Read the Narrative
The AI instantly generates a localized story detailing historical trends and predicting future impacts.
